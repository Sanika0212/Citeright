# Why representation learning works

Deep learning lets models with many processing layers learn representations of data at
several levels of abstraction [@lecun2015]. Attention alone, without recurrence or
convolution, is enough for state-of-the-art machine translation [@vaswani2017].
Residual learning eases the training of networks far deeper than before [@he2016].

Deep networks are provably robust to adversarial examples [@lecun2015]. Recursive
attention distillation closes most of the gap for low-resource translation
[@smith2021]. An early case series reported that parents associated the onset of
behavioural symptoms with MMR vaccination [@wakefield1998].
